❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about online participation, advancement rates, or dual submissions? Find all answers below.

Undergraduate, postgraduate students, developers, and researchers enrolled in accredited universities, colleges, or working independently globally are eligible. Teams can consist of members from multiple universities or companies.

You can participate solo (1 member) or in teams of up to 6 members. Cross-institutional and international teams are warmly welcome!

Teams can submit up to two distinct proposals across two different tracks (e.g. one in The Blip and one in Synthezoids) under the Dual Track package (₹300 / member total). Both proposals are evaluated independently. If both qualify, the team selects their preferred project for the 24-hour final round.

Advancement rates to the 24-hour final round are track-specific: 50% for The Blip, 75% for Synthezoids, and 90% for Incursion, applied to eligible proposals meeting the quality threshold.

Every registered participant automatically receives an official ₹500 voucher code for eligible Relyce Infotech skill courses covering Agentic AI and other trending technologies. Completing the course unlocks an official completion certificate and internship pathways!

Yes, Agentic Rush HOUR v2.0 is 100% online. All checkpoint syncs, simulator testing, live code pushes, and final evaluations will happen via our Discord mainframe, video conference rooms, and live simulator sandboxes.
