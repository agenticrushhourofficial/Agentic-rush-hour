📅 24-HOUR SPRINT & TIMELINE

Schedule of Events

Prepare yourself for 24 hours of autonomous agent orchestration, milestone checkpoints, and live adversarial challenges. Here is your roadmap.

08:30 – 09:30 AM

🌐 Global Virtual Onboarding & Keynote

Online participant verification on the hackathon portal, platform orientation, and opening keynote by Sathyabama & Relyce Infotech leadership.

09:30 AM

💎 Stone Prompt Release & API Credentials

Official release of problem statements across The Blip (6 Infinity Stones), Synthezoids, and Incursion. Model credits & sandbox environments unlocked.

10:00 AM

⚡ 24-Hour Countdown Commences — Sprint Begins!

The 24-hour hackathon clock starts ticking. Teams orchestrate multi-agent graphs, memory layers, tool contracts, and autonomous workflows.

01:00 PM

🍽️ Midday Recharge & AI Tech Talk

Lunch pause accompanied by an exclusive fireside session on Multi-Agent Resilience by Relyce Infotech AI research engineers.

03:00 PM (Hour 6)

🔍 Milestone Review 1 — Agent Topology & Architecture

First mentor checkpoint: evaluation of agent orchestration graphs (LangGraph/AutoGen/CrewAI), planning-action-verification loops, and schema definitions.

06:30 PM

☕ Virtual Tech Lounge & Mentor Office Hours

Short refreshment break. Open technical office hours with mentors for API debugging and system integration tips.

📊 Milestone Review 2 — Midpoint Simulation & Stress Test

Second evaluation round assessing agent reasoning accuracy, tool execution success rates, and resilience under synthetic workloads. Scoreboard updated.

🛠️ Self-Healing & Fallback Hardening

09:00 AM – 10:00 AM

02:00 – 03:30 PM

Declaration of winners, presentation of ₹50,000 cash prizes, Relyce Infotech ₹500 course vouchers, internship awards, and Avengers: Doomsday tickets!

Teams of 1–6 register for Single Submission (₹200 / $5) or Dual Submission (₹300 / $7). Choose between The Blip, Synthezoids, or Incursion and submit preliminary architectural proposal.

🔑 Phase 3: Finalist Confirmation & Sandbox Access

Advancing teams receive confirmation, agentic sandbox API access, DOOM Protocol briefing packets, and final instructions for the Nov 10 24-hour sprint.
